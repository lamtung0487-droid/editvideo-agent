## Images, models, maps and terrain

- **Earth, "Blue Marble" texture**: NASA Visible Earth ([visibleearth.nasa.gov](https://visibleearth.nasa.gov)), public domain.
- **Moon surface**: a procedural texture generated by EditVideo. It is an illustration, not a lunar map. The sizes
  and the distance in chapter 03 are to scale.
- **BoomBox model**: Microsoft, from the [Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/BoomBox), CC0.
- **Terrain of the opening**:
  - Elevation from NASA SRTM (public domain).
  - Imagery from [Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016](https://s2maps.eu) by EOX IT Services GmbH, which contains modified
    Copernicus Sentinel data 2016, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The imagery is
    draped on the terrain and rendered in Blender.
- **Bloc map**: country outlines from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) (public domain), extruded and
  rendered in Blender.
